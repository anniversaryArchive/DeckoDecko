DeckoDecko Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · 한국어

This is an English translation of the Korean privacy policy. If the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Principles

DeckoDecko (가챠 탐색대, the "App") can be used without signing up or logging in. Your WISH and GET records are stored only on your device.

Our server has no feature that collects your name, email address or phone number. The only information the App sends to the server is listed in Section 2.

2. Information we process and why

The App processes the information below only for the purposes listed.

When Information Purpose
When you open a gacha detail screen A random device identifier (UUID) created by the App, the gacha you viewed, and the date Counting popular gacha and preventing duplicate counts (one per device, per gacha, per day)
When you search for gacha Your search terms Returning search results (search terms are not stored on the server)
When you send a request or inquiry Device identifier, inquiry type, title and message, series name, attached photo path, and App version Reviewing and handling your inquiry, and limiting daily submissions (5 per device)
When you contact us by email Your email address and message Handling inquiries and rights requests
When the App communicates with the server Your IP address and access records may be kept in our service provider's logs Operating and securing the service
When you open the App and it checks for updates A random device identifier created by the App for updates (separate from the one above), operating system, App version, and IP address Delivering App updates

Please do not include personal information, such as your name or contact details, in inquiries.

3. Information stored only on your device

Your WISH and GET records, folders, photos added to records, recent searches, recently viewed gacha, and language setting are stored only on your device and are never sent to the server.

When you delete the App, this information and the device identifiers are removed from your device. They cannot be restored, even if you reinstall the App.

The App copies only the photos you choose into its own folder. It does not read your whole photo library and does not use your location, contacts, or advertising identifier.

4. Retention and deletion

Information on the server is permanently deleted when the period below ends or when its purpose has been fulfilled.

Information Retention period
Gacha view records 30 days from collection
Requests and inquiries 1 year after the inquiry is resolved
Email inquiries 1 year after the inquiry is resolved
Service provider access logs Deleted automatically according to Supabase's retention policy
App update check records Deleted automatically according to Expo's retention policy

5. Service providers and international transfers

We use Supabase (Tokyo region, Japan) to run our server and Expo (United States) to deliver App updates. We do not provide your information to any other third party.

Recipient Country Information transferred When and how Retention
Supabase Pte. Ltd. (privacy@supabase.com) Japan (Tokyo region) Information sent to the server in Section 2, except update checks Sent over the network while you use the App Same as Section 4
650 Industries, Inc. (Expo, privacy@expo.dev) United States Update check information in Section 2 Sent over the network when you open the App Same as Section 4

If you do not want your information transferred abroad, you can delete the App. You will then be unable to use it.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your information. Send your request to the email address in Section 7, and we will handle it without delay after verifying it.

Because there are no accounts, we may ask for details such as the date and content of your inquiry to confirm that a server record belongs to you. You can delete records stored on your device yourself in the App, or by deleting the App.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that an inquiry from a child contains personal information, we will delete it.
